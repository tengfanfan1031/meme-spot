8 關真實相片，每關兩張圖有 3 個不同，全場約 5 分鐘。大家同時玩同一關，越快找齊越高分。點錯會凍結 3 秒。
8 rounds of real photos, 3 differences each, about 5 minutes in total. Everyone plays the same round at the same time. Find all 5 faster to score more. A wrong tap freezes you for 3 seconds.
